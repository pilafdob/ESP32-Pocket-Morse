THE TWO-BUTTON LANGUAGE

Dots, dashes, then send.

Tap DOT or DASH for Morse; a letter auto-confirms after 0.5 seconds. Tap BOTH to confirm sooner, or to add a space when no letter is pending. A blinking underline marks the next character position in the draft. Hold both for 0.8 seconds to send.

Hold DASH to erase a pending or last letter, hold DOT to erase the last word. Double-tap BOTH to open the inbox, including saved read messages; triple-tap BOTH to recall your last sent message when the draft is empty; quadruple-tap BOTH to open the Morse dictionary. DOT/DASH change its pages, BOTH closes it. On receipt, tap BOTH to reveal; DOT/DASH browse saved messages; hold DOT while reading to delete that message immediately. Tap BOTH to compose again. The screen shows the current message number and estimated remaining inbox slots with a safety reserve. The little dial shows auto-confirm and hold progress. Failed sends keep the draft. LINK means a recent authenticated peer reply.
